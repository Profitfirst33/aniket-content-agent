# nick_saraev reel style (from frame-by-frame study, 31 Oct 2026)

Sources: reels DfFlmTrvYcr (Genjutsu, 32 s) and DezZdo0Pf5r (One API, 34 s).

## Layout (1080x1920)
| Zone | y range | What |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | 150-330 | Line 1: black bold sans ("Put Yourself In"). Line 2: **lime box** (#D9F23E) with **black italic serif** ("Any Viral Video"). Words appear one by one. |
| Demo card | 340-760 | Rounded 28px card, **lime 5px border + soft lime glow**. Holds a video, a screenshot, or a website inside a **browser chrome mock** (traffic lights, URL bar). |
| Caption | 780-880 | 1-3 words, centred, heavy sans ~86px. Black on light background, white on dark. **Keywords in lime italic serif** (with a dark outline on light bg). |
| Face | 1000-1920 | Talking head at the bottom, sitting in a **rounded light-grey card** (#D6D8DA) from ~y 1250. The head **pops out above the card** (cut-out). |

## Backgrounds
- **Light:** off-white paper (#EDEFEE) with fine grain + a soft **palm-leaf shadow** top-left.
- **Dark:** the demo clip itself, scaled up and heavily blurred, darkened. Used for "wow" demo moments and lists.

## Scene types seen
1. **Hook (0-3 s):** headline + demo clip + caption + face. Everything on screen at once.
2. **Tool intro:** lime headline box alone + browser-mock website. Face hidden.
3. **Compare stack:** two demo cards stacked (INPUT / OUTPUT label chips top-right) on dark blurred bg, caption below.
4. **UI walkthrough:** browser/app mock with a **cursor that moves and clicks**, element highlight in lime.
5. **List scroll:** a stack of lime-bordered cards scrolling up on dark bg (model names in mono font).
6. **Chat demo:** Claude app window mock with messages and status rows with ✅.
7. **CTA end:** full-screen face, caption "comment **"Storm"**" with lime glow.

## Motion
- Something new every **1.5-3 s**.
- Cards enter with **scale 0.92→1 + blur 12→0 + fade**, ~0.35 s, ease-out.
- Transitions between sections: **blur dissolve** or quick white wash.
- Slow push-in (Ken Burns 1.00→1.06) on demo media.
- Captions pop word by word (scale 0.85→1).
- Cursor: smooth path, click = small scale pulse + lime ripple.

## Type
- Sans: Inter / Helvetica Neue, weight 800-900.
- Serif italic for accents: Instrument Serif Italic / DM Serif Display Italic.
- Mono for model names / code: JetBrains Mono.

## Colours
- Lime #D9F23E (box, borders, keyword text)
- Ink #0E0E0E, paper #EDEFEE, face card #D6D8DA, dark bg = blurred clip at 45% brightness
